Navigation

page-transition

runtime

An animated cover between pages of any multi-page site, WordPress and Webflow included. It covers when a link is clicked and reveals the next page as it arrives, with no flash in between. Single-page apps call lumafx.page.cover() and reveal().

On a real site this plays between pages automatically.

<div data-fx="page-transition" data-fx-style="wave" data-fx-label="Sphere"></div>

preloader

runtime

The intro you saw when this page opened: a 000 → 100 counter that follows real loading, then lifts away with a wave. Entrance animations wait for it, so they play as the curtain rises. It removes itself after a few seconds even if JavaScript fails.

<div data-fx="preloader" data-fx-style="wave"><span data-fx-count></span></div>

runtime

A real <dialog> with focus handling, Esc and a blurred backdrop. The liquid pulls out of whatever opened it, like a drop that stretches and detaches, then blooms into the panel; closing flows back into the same button. Try these, the header's Join button, or the green icon in the dock below.

runtime
